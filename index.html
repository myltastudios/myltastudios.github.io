DR. MATBEN

Survive the simulation. Save the world.

Dr. Matben vs The AI

When the brilliant (but perhaps careless) Dr. Matben accidentally creates a rogue artificial intelligence intent on destroying the world, there's only one way out.

Dive into a simulation of the doctor's own memories to find a way to stop the world's destruction. Every run is unique in this fast-paced 2.5D action roguelite.

Shoot, platform, and loop your way through a hostile sci-fi world until the system crashes – or you do.

Operational Intel

Independent Core

Founded in Umeå, Sweden by Henrik and Linus. We build the games we want to play, without compromise.

100% Human Art

Every frame of pixel art is meticulously hand-drawn by our lead artist, Linus. Zero AI generation used, just pure artistic intent and bespoke sci-fi design.

Rigorously Optimized

We push the visual boundaries of what a 2.5D roguelite can look like, but smooth performance is extremely important. DR. MATBEN is heavily optimized to deliver striking, atmospheric graphics without sacrificing framerates.

Kick-Ass Audio

Featuring an awesome, high-energy original soundtrack composed in-house by Henrik Öberg.

Endless Replayability

Every dive into Dr. Matben's simulated memories is completely unique. Dynamic layouts, randomized upgrades, and unpredictable AI behaviors mean no two runs are ever the same.

Lovable Cast

Despite the seriousness of the situation, the world is full of heart. Encounter a quirky, personal cast of memorable characters who will aid, mock, and guide you through the madness.
