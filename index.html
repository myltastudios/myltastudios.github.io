DR. MATBEN

Survive the simulation. Save the world.

Dr. Matben vs The AI

The brilliant (but perhaps careless) Dr. Matben made a mistake.

After creating an artificial intelligence he can no longer control, the only way to stop it is to dive back into fragmented simulations built from his own memories.

Shoot, platform, and loop your way through a hostile digital world and uncover what went wrong in this fast-paced 2.5D action roguelite.

The Game

Independent Studio

MYLTA Studios is a small indie team based in northern Sweden, founded by Henrik and Supis. We build the games we want to play, without compromise.

Supis

Henrik

100% Handcrafted Art

All art in DR. MATBEN is drawn by hand. No AI-generated assets are used in the game.

Built For Performance

We care a lot about responsiveness and smooth gameplay. DR. MATBEN is heavily optimized to keep combat fast and fluid without sacrificing fidelity, even on lower-end hardware.

Original Soundtrack

The retro-inspired soundtrack is composed and produced in-house by Henrik.

Endless Replayability

Areas, upgrades, and encounters change between runs, forcing you to adapt as the simulations change.

Strange Characters

The world of DR. MATBEN is filled with unusual personalities. Some helpful, some hostile, and some impossible to fully trust.
