An action-platformer roguelite about a 99 year old genius.


He Built It to Save Humanity

Dr. Matben created an AI to save humanity from an impending ice age. It found a solution. Now humanity is in danger.

Customize your Modgun with powerful mods, explore simulations of your memories, and fight through a hostile digital world to uncover the key to stopping it.
